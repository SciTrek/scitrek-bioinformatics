SciTrek Bioinformatics

How to Create Your Account & Log In

A short guide for students. Please read this before your first class session.

1. Open the website

Go to: https://bioinformatics-mod.sci-trek.org

2. Create a new account (first time only)

  1. Click Sign Up at the top of the login box.
  2. Enter a username that is unique to you.
  3. Create a strong password (see rules below).
  4. Type the same password again in Confirm Password.
  5. Enter your first name and last name.
  6. Enter your classroom name exactly as your teacher gives it to you (for example, 1001).
  7. Click Sign Up.

Example that works:

  • Username: jordan.lee25
  • Password: SciTrek-Classroom-2026!
  • Classroom: 1001 (use the code your teacher gives you)

You can invent your own password. The example above is only a model of length and complexity — do not reuse a password you already use for email or school accounts.

3. Password rules (important)

Your password must be:

  • At least 8 characters long (longer is better)
  • Not a common word or easy guess (avoid password, 123456, your name, or your birthday)
  • Not only numbers

Good ideas:

  • Mix uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and a symbol
  • Use a short phrase you can remember, then add numbers/symbols
  • Write it down somewhere safe if your teacher allows it for class accounts

Usually rejected:

  • password, abc123, qwerty
  • Your first name or username by itself
  • Very short passwords like pass or sci

4. Why do we need a longer password?

SciTrek stores your class work, answers, and progress. A short or common password is easy for automated programs (and curious classmates) to guess.

A longer, less common password helps protect:

  • Your workbook answers and quiz progress
  • Messages and files in your account
  • The fairness of the classroom activity (so someone else cannot pretend to be you)

This is the same idea used for school email and banking apps: longer passwords are much harder to crack, even if someone tries many guesses quickly.

5. Log in next time

  1. Open the same website.
  2. Stay on Sign In (not Sign Up).
  3. Enter the same username and password you created.
  4. Click Sign In.

Tip: If you forget your password, ask your teacher for help. There is no self-serve “forgot password” link yet.

6. Quick checklist before class

  • I can open the SciTrek website
  • I created a unique username
  • My password is long and not a common word
  • I saved or remembered my username and password
  • I know my classroom name/code from my teacher
  • I successfully signed in once before class starts

7. If something goes wrong

  • “Sign up failed” / password error: Make a longer, less common password and try again.
  • “Too many signup attempts” / throttled: Wait about 15–20 minutes, then try one time with a strong password. Many failed tries from the same school network can temporarily pause signup.
  • Classroom does not exist: Double-check the classroom name with your teacher (spelling and numbers matter).
  • Username already taken: Add numbers or your class period, such as jordan.lee.p3.
